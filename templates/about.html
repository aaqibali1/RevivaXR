About RevivaXR

Inspiration

Patients in long-term care often face repetitive routines that hinder their recovery. RevivaXR aims to break this cycle with gamified and immersive XR therapy.

Our Journey

Using Unity, SnapDragon Spaces SDK, and ThinkReality smart glasses, we developed a calming multi-scene experience to enhance emotional and physical well-being.

Challenges

Aligning diverse ideas, translating concepts into actionable features, and integrating interactable elements into unfamiliar XR hardware were key challenges.
